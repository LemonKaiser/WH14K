# WH40K Meta Reward Distribution 1-40

This file documents the rebuilt WH40K account reward distribution after the 40-level progression rebalance.

- Levels 2-40 grant `3` decoration rewards in the preview table and `1` skill point.
- Level 1 keeps only the starting baseline rewards.
- Gradient colors and animated title effects are intentionally concentrated in the later progression bands, mostly from level 20+.
- `decor-title-discord-envoy` and `decor-color-discord-blurple` additionally require a linked Discord account and membership in the configured Discord guild.
- `decor-title-fish` additionally requires the Discord role `1479487752960737505`.
- Only `decor-ghost-pack-icon` stays admin-only in the reward manifest.
